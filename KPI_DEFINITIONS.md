# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Sum of FactSales revenue |
| Total Cost | Sum of FactSales cost |
| Gross Profit | Sum of FactSales gross profit |
| Gross Margin % | Gross Profit divided by Revenue |
| Total Orders | Distinct count of OrderID |
| Completed Orders | Distinct count of orders with Completed status |
| Returned Orders | Distinct count of orders with Returned status |
| Cancelled Orders | Distinct count of orders with Cancelled status |
| Units Sold | Quantity excluding cancelled orders |
| AOV | Revenue divided by completed orders |
| Customers | Distinct count of CustomerID |
| Target Revenue | Sum of FactTargets target revenue |
| Target Variance | Revenue minus Target Revenue |
| Target Achievement % | Revenue divided by Target Revenue |
| Revenue Growth % | Current-period revenue change versus prior month |
| Revenue per Customer | Revenue divided by distinct customers |
| Inventory Value | Sum of inventory value |
| Stock On Hand | Sum of stock on hand |
| Low Stock Items | Distinct products with Low Stock status |
| Stockout Items | Distinct products with Stockout status |
| Stockout Rate % | Stockout products divided by distinct inventory products |
| Inventory Turnover | Sales cost divided by inventory value |
| Units Sold per Stock Unit | Units sold divided by stock on hand |

All definitions are based on the project semantic model and source measures; no values are fabricated for presentation purposes.
